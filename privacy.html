Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Plain English summary: We collect your email and survey answers to generate your report. We don't sell your data. We don't spam you. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

1. Who We Are

CyprusReady is a digital product that provides personalised relocation reports for digital nomads planning stays in Cyprus. When we say "we", "us", or "CyprusReady" in this policy, we're referring to the CyprusReady service.

For questions about this policy, contact us at: cyprusready@gmail.com

2. What Information We Collect

When you use CyprusReady, we collect the following information:

  • Email address — provided during payment and survey. Used to deliver your report.
  • Survey responses — your answers to the 25-question relocation survey. Used solely to generate your personalised report.
  • Payment information — processed by Stripe. We do not store your card details. Stripe's privacy policy applies to payment processing.
  • Waitlist email — if you join our waitlist, we store your email address to notify you at launch.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes only:

  • To generate and deliver your personalised relocation report
  • To email you your report and any follow-up correspondence related to your purchase
  • To notify waitlist members when CyprusReady launches
  • To improve the quality of our reports over time

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to delivering your report.

4. How We Store Your Information

Your survey responses and email address are stored in Google Sheets and Google Drive, both of which are subject to Google's security and privacy standards. Your report is stored as a Google Doc and shared with you via a private link.

We take reasonable steps to protect your information but no internet transmission is 100% secure.

5. Third Parties

We use the following third-party services to operate CyprusReady:

  • Stripe — payment processing. Stripe's privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy
  • OpenAI — report generation. Your survey answers are sent to OpenAI's API to generate your report. OpenAI's privacy policy: openai.com/privacy
  • Google — Forms, Sheets, Docs, and Gmail. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any other third parties.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Request that we delete your data
  • Unsubscribe from any emails at any time
  • Ask questions about how your data is used

To exercise any of these rights, email us at cyprusready@gmail.com and we will respond within 14 days.

7. Cookies

Our website does not use cookies or tracking scripts. We do not use analytics tools or advertising pixels on cyprusready.com.

8. Children

CyprusReady is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of CyprusReady after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at cyprusready@gmail.com. We will respond within 14 days.
