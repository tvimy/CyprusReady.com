Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Who we are

CyprusReady operates at cyprusready.it.com. We provide personalised relocation reports for digital nomads planning to move to Cyprus. You can contact us at cyprusready@gmail.com.

What data we collect and why

We collect only what is necessary to deliver your report:

  • Survey responses — your answers to the 25-question relocation survey (budget, work setup, lifestyle preferences, citizenship). Used solely to generate your personalised report.
  • Email address — collected via the survey form to deliver your completed report. We do not send marketing emails.
  • Payment information — processed entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.

Third-party services

We use the following services to operate CyprusReady:

  • Stripe — payment processing. Stripe’s privacy policy applies to all payment data: stripe.com/privacy
  • Google Forms & Google Sheets — survey collection and data storage. Google’s privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy
  • OpenAI — your survey responses are processed by OpenAI’s API to generate your report. Data is not used for model training. OpenAI’s privacy policy: openai.com/privacy
  • Google Docs & Gmail — your report is created as a Google Doc and delivered to your email address via Gmail.

How long we retain your data

Your survey responses and email address are retained in Google Sheets for up to 12 months, after which they are deleted. Your delivered report document remains accessible via its Google Docs link indefinitely unless you request deletion.

Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, email us at cyprusready@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Cookies

CyprusReady does not use tracking cookies or analytics on this website. Stripe may set cookies during the payment process; these are governed by Stripe’s own policy.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance.
